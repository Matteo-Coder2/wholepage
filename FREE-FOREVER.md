# The Free Forever contract

This file is the product's pricing constitution. Moving anything OUT of this list
is a breaking promise and would be visible as a git commit in a public repository —
that is the point. It can only ever grow.

**Free forever, for everyone, with no account, no watermark, and no usage caps:**

- Full-page capture (including lazy-loaded content and app-shell inner scrollers)
- Visible-area capture
- Drag-select area capture
- Copy to clipboard
- Save as PNG
- Save as multi-page PDF (automatic splitting for long pages, no branding, no hijacked links)
- Crop
- Filename templates
- Keyboard shortcuts
- Every capture-quality behavior: sticky-header neutralization, animation freeze,
  high-DPI-exact stitching, honest handling of oversized pages
- The annotation editor, when it ships (arrows, text, shapes, highlight, blur/redact,
  step markers) — stated here in advance so there is no ambiguity

**What may ever cost money (one-time payment, never a subscription):**

- Batch capture of a URL list with filename mapping
- Scheduled/automated captures
- Bulk export tooling

**Never, at any price:**

- Watermarks or vendor branding on any output
- Accounts or sign-in requirements
- Cloud upload of captures without an explicit per-capture action
- Telemetry, analytics, or tracking of any kind
- Nag popups, trial timers, "New!" badges, or update tabs
- Sale or transfer of this extension or its user base — inbound acquisition offers
  are treated as threat signals, not opportunities (see the category's history:
  The Great Suspender, Nimbus, Blipshot)
