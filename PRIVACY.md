# Privacy policy

WholePage collects nothing.

Screenshots are captured, assembled, and saved entirely on your device. The
extension makes **zero network requests** — no analytics, no telemetry, no
crash reporting, no update pings, no fonts or scripts from CDNs, no cloud.
There is no account system. Nothing you capture ever leaves your machine.

This is verifiable, not just promised: the extension requests no host
permissions (so it has no standing ability to talk to any website), the full
source code is public, and you can open DevTools during any capture and observe
that no requests are made.

The `activeTab` permission means WholePage can only see a page at the moment you
explicitly invoke it on that page — it cannot read your browsing, and it has no
access to any tab you didn't capture.

If this policy ever changes, the change will be visible in the public repository
history and called out in the changelog before it ships. The intended direction
of this policy is: it never changes.
